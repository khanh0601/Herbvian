With proper daily care, one natural Herbvian loofah lasts 6 to 8 weeks for daily body showers, and up to 3 to 4 months when used as an eco kitchen dish scrubber. We recommend replacing it when fibers naturally soften and lose their resilient spring.

Rinse thoroughly under running warm water after every shower. Gently press flat between your palms (avoid twisting), then hang it in a well-ventilated dry area by its cotton loop. To sanitize every 2 weeks, dip in boiling water for 2 minutes or soak in a mild water-vinegar solution.

Yes, 100% backyard compostable! Unlike synthetic plastic poufs that persist for hundreds of years, Herbvian loofahs are pure dried fruit xylem from the Luffa Aegyptiaca vine. Snip the cotton hanging cord, cut into pieces, and bury in your compost pile. It decomposes into organic plant nutrition in 30–45 days.
